# 🎬 Journey Planner Demo Guide

## Quick Demo: Mumbai to Goa

### Step 1: Access the Journey Planner
```
URL: http://localhost:5173/journey/new
```

### Step 2: Enter Origin
1. Click on "Origin" input field
2. Type: `Mumbai`
3. Wait for suggestions to appear
4. Click on: `Mumbai, Maharashtra, India`

### Step 3: Enter Destination
1. Click on "Destination" input field
2. Type: `Goa`
3. Wait for suggestions to appear
4. Click on: `Goa, India`

### Step 4: Select Travel Mode
- Click on 🚗 **Car** (default)
- Or try 🚴 **Bike** or 🚶 **Walk**

### Step 5: Plan Journey
1. Click the **"Plan Journey"** button
2. Wait 2-3 seconds for:
   - Route calculation
   - Weather forecast
   - Map rendering

### Step 6: Explore Results

#### Map View
- **Green marker (A)** - Your origin (Mumbai)
- **Red marker (B)** - Your destination (Goa)
- **Blue line** - Your route
- **Blue dots** - Turn points (click for instructions)

#### Route Summary
- Distance: ~440 km
- Duration: ~7 hours
- Mode: Driving

#### Weather Card
- Current temperature in Goa
- Weather condition (sunny, cloudy, etc.)
- Humidity, wind speed
- Weather icon

#### Route Instructions
- Step-by-step directions
- Distance for each step
- Duration for each step
- Turn-by-turn navigation

## Features to Try

### 1. Click Markers
- Click the **green origin marker** to see origin details
- Click the **red destination marker** to see destination details
- Click **blue route dots** to see turn instructions

### 2. Zoom and Pan
- **Scroll** to zoom in/out
- **Click and drag** to pan around
- **Double-click** to zoom in quickly

### 3. Change Travel Mode
- Switch between car, bike, and walk
- Click "Plan Journey" again to recalculate
- Notice how the route changes!

### 4. Try Different Locations
- **Cities**: New York, London, Tokyo
- **Landmarks**: Eiffel Tower, Taj Mahal, Statue of Liberty
- **Addresses**: Full street addresses work too!

## Tips for Best Experience

### 1. Be Specific
❌ Bad: "India"
✅ Good: "Mumbai, India"

### 2. Wait for Suggestions
- Type at least 3 characters
- Wait 1 second for results
- Select from dropdown

### 3. Check Weather
- Weather shows destination conditions
- Plan accordingly for rain, heat, etc.
- Check "feels like" temperature

### 4. Review Route Steps
- Read all instructions before starting
- Note major turns and highways
- Check total distance and time

### 5. Try Different Modes
- Car: Fastest, uses highways
- Bike: Bike-friendly routes
- Walk: Pedestrian paths

## Troubleshooting Demo

### Issue: No Suggestions
**Solution:** Type more characters (at least 3)

### Issue: Route Not Showing
**Solution:** Wait 2-3 seconds, check internet

### Issue: Map Blank
**Solution:** Refresh page, check browser console

### Issue: Wrong Location
**Solution:** Be more specific in search
