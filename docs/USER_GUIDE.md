## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Click the "Book Now" button on any hotel
2. If you don't have an account, you'll be prompted to create one
3. Enter your email and create a password
4. Verify your email address

### Logging In

1. Click on the "Profile" icon in the navigation bar
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click "Sign In"

## Searching for Hotels

### Basic Search

1. Enter your destination in the search bar on the home page
2. Select your check-in and check-out dates
3. Specify the number of guests
4. Click "Search"

### Using Vibe Filters

The Vibe sidebar allows you to customize your search based on preferences:

- **Energy Level**: Quiet retreat (1) to Vibrant nightlife (10)
- **Social Atmosphere**: Private escape (1) to Social hub (10)
- **Budget**: Budget-friendly (1) to Luxury (10)

Adjust the sliders to find hotels that match your vibe.

### Instant Booking Filter

Toggle "Instant Booking Only" to see hotels that can be booked immediately without waiting for confirmation.

## Making a Booking

### Step 1: Select Your Hotel

1. Browse the hotel listings
2. Click on a hotel card to view details
3. Click "Book Now" to start the booking process

### Step 2: Choose Your Dates and Room

1. Select check-in and check-out dates
2. Choose the number of guests
3. Select your preferred room type:
   - Standard
   - Deluxe
   - Suite

### Step 3: Enter Guest Information

Provide the following details:
- First Name
- Last Name
- Email Address
- Phone Number
- Special Requests (optional)

### Step 4: Review and Pay

1. Review your booking details
2. Check the pricing summary:
   - Room rate per night
   - Number of nights
   - Taxes and fees
   - Total amount
3. Enter payment information
4. Click "Complete Booking"

### Step 5: Confirmation

After successful payment:
- You'll receive a confirmation number
- A confirmation email will be sent
- You can download your booking details as PDF
- Add the booking to your calendar

## Managing Your Bookings

### Viewing Booking History

1. Click "My Bookings" in the navigation bar
2. View all your past and upcoming bookings
3. Filter by status:
   - Upcoming
   - Completed
   - Cancelled

### Booking Details

Click on any booking to view:
- Hotel information
- Check-in/check-out dates
- Guest details
- Payment information
- Confirmation number

## Modifying a Booking

### Changing Dates or Guests

1. Go to "My Bookings"
2. Select the booking you want to modify
3. Click "Modify Booking"
4. Update your dates or number of guests
5. Review the price difference
6. Confirm the changes

**Note**: Price differences will be charged or refunded based on availability and hotel policies.

### Modification Policies

- Free modifications up to 48 hours before check-in
- Modifications within 48 hours may incur fees
- Some promotional rates may not be modifiable

## Cancelling a Booking

### Cancellation Process

1. Go to "My Bookings"
2. Select the booking you want to cancel
3. Click "Cancel Booking"
4. Select a cancellation reason
5. Review the refund amount
6. Confirm cancellation

### Refund Policy

- **Free Cancellation**: Full refund if cancelled 48+ hours before check-in
- **Late Cancellation**: 50% refund if cancelled 24-48 hours before check-in
- **No-Show**: No refund for no-shows or same-day cancellations

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to your original payment method.

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Booking Not Confirmed

**Problem**: Payment processed but no confirmation received

**Solution**:
1. Check your email spam folder
2. Go to "My Bookings" to verify the booking status
3. Contact support with your booking reference

#### Payment Failed

**Problem**: Payment declined or error during checkout

**Solution**:
1. Verify your card details are correct
2. Check with your bank for any blocks
3. Try a different payment method
4. Contact support if the issue persists

#### Can't Modify Booking

**Problem**: Modification option is disabled

**Solution**:
1. Check if you're within the modification window
2. Some promotional rates can't be modified
3. Contact the hotel directly for special requests

#### Refund Not Received

**Problem**: Cancelled booking but refund not processed

**Solution**:
1. Refunds take 5-7 business days
2. Check your original payment method
3. Contact support with your cancellation reference
